About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher at Peking University, focusing on medical artificial intelligence and biomedical signal analysis.

My research interests include biomedical foundation models, representation learning, physiological signal processing, and generalizable machine learning methods for healthcare applications.

I am particularly interested in developing robust and reliable AI methods that can bridge machine learning and clinical medicine.

ECG-LabWAS

A laboratory-wide association study using a fine-tuned single-lead ECG foundation model.

The study investigates associations between ECG signals and laboratory abnormalities through multi-task learning and independent external validation.
